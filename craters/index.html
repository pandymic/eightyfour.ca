Aim, set your power, fire

A turn-based artillery game in the spirit of the classics. You get a tiny tank, a big-headed driver with a lot of attitude, and a landscape made of voxels that comes apart a little more with every shot. Last tank standing wins.

  • Every shot carves a real crater. Tunnel through hills, undermine towers, drop a cave roof on somebody.
  • Drive, climb and jump before you shoot. Stamina decides how far you get.
  • Thirteen weapons, from a plain shell to a MIRV that splits into five warheads.
  • Crates parachute in every turn. Repairs, shields, nitro and bigger guns, for whoever reaches them first.
  • 2 to 4 players: pass one phone around, play CPUs on three difficulty levels, or go online.
  • Four stages, generated fresh every match. No ads. No in-app purchases. Just craters.

One turn

Read the wind gauge. Drag to aim. Hold FIRE and watch the two arcs of the power gauge fill, then let go. The shell leaves the barrel, the breeze leans on it, and everybody at the table holds their breath until it lands.

Then the ground gives way, the damage numbers fly, and it’s the next driver’s turn to get even.

Blow up the world

  • Everything is voxels, so everything breaks: hills, pillboxes, buildings, jungle ruins.
  • Knock a leg out from under a steel antenna tower and it topples, away from the blast, and lies where it falls.
  • Red crates are TNT. Hit one and it sets off the next.
  • Overhangs crumble. Rubble falls, piles up, and hurts anyone underneath it.
  • Blasts shove tanks around. Get knocked off a cliff and the fall counts too.

Weapons for every plan

You start with trusty Shells, Grenades that bounce, and a Cluster Bomb. The rest comes by parachute.

  • Shotgun for close quarters, and the Flamethrower for closer.
  • Airstrike and Napalm Strike: throw a flare, then get out of the way.
  • Homing Missile: chases the nearest enemy.
  • Mortar and Driller: over the hill, or straight through it.
  • MIRV: one shot up, five warheads down.
  • Mines wait for someone to drive too close. The Teleport pearl gets you out of trouble.

  • Supply and weapon crates

    Extra rounds, or a weapon you didn’t have.

  • Repair kits

    Get some health back before the next shell arrives.

  • Shield bubble

    Soaks up damage for three turns.

  • Nitro and tonics

    Nitro makes driving faster and cheaper for two turns. A tonic refills your stamina.

  • Overcharge

    Your next shot hits half again as hard.

  • Windbreaker

    Your next two shots ignore the breeze.

Wind, weather and physics

A breeze blows across every match. It changes strength from turn to turn and gusts while a shot is in the air, so the gauge at the top of the screen is worth a look before you fire. Hair, smoke and flags all lean with it.

Drag to aim, hold FIRE to charge, release to let fly. Two fingers pan and zoom the battlefield at any time, and arrows at the edge of the screen point to every driver who is out of view.

Chibi drivers, cute tanks

  • Twenty big-headed pixel drivers, each with six skin tones and six hair dyes. They blink, they react, and their hair blows in the wind.
  • Sixteen paint jobs: plain colours, camo, racing stripes, tiger stripes, hazard stripes and more.
  • Your flag flies your colour, or your team’s. Pick a crosshair while you’re at it: nine styles, eight colours.
  • Can’t decide? Roll a random loadout for one seat, or for everyone.

Four stages

Every match generates its battlefield from scratch, with caves, cliffs and things to hide behind. Pick a stage in the lobby, or leave it to chance.

  • Plains

    Rolling green hills over stone and caves. Antenna towers stand on the ridges, waiting for someone to cut a leg.

  • Desert

    Sandstone mesas and red-rock strata. High ground is everything, until somebody removes it.

  • Urban

    City blocks of brick, concrete and steel. Drive into a building for cover and hope it’s still there next turn.

  • Jungle

    Steep, cave-riddled ridges and old ruins under the trees. Nothing here is level.

Play your way

  • Pass and play. 2 to 4 players on one device, taking turns.
  • CPU opponents on Easy, Normal and Hard. Easy forgets about the wind. Hard doesn’t.
  • Free-for-all or teams, Gold against Cyan, with friendly fire off between teammates.
  • Sudden death, if you want it. At the turn you choose, space lasers sweep the battlefield and everyone drops to 1 HP.
  • Online with friends, on a server you or a friend runs: lobbies, password-locked rooms, optional chat and CPU seats. There is no official server, and the game never goes online until you type an address in.
  • Bring your own drivers and vehicles. Resource packs are plain files, and they stay on your device.

Credits

  • The tank: Tank Pack by Batuhan Karagöl (Andel Rodis).
  • The drivers: Pixel Characters by hacked.design.

  • Fonts: Silkscreen and Pixelify Sans, both under the SIL Open Font License.
