Chibi Craters

Turn-based voxel artillery: chibi drivers, tiny tanks and big explosions.

Aim, set your power, fire

A turn-based artillery game in the spirit of the classics, with fully destructible voxel terrain, big-headed chibi drivers and tiny tanks with a lot of attitude.

  • Blow the ground apart. Chunky voxel terrain shatters, collapses and burns.
  • Pick a driver and a ride, then drive, jump and grab power-ups before you take your shot.
  • 2 to 4 players: pass one phone around, play CPUs at three difficulty levels, or go online through your own server.
  • Four stages: plains, desert, urban and jungle. No ads, no in-app purchases.
