A lightweight BitTorrent client

Paste a magnet link or open a .torrent file, pick a destination folder, and Simpleech downloads it there. When the transfer reaches 100% it disconnects from the tracker and peers on its own.

  • One job at a time: no queues and no multi-torrent management to look after.
  • Choose where it goes: pick the destination folder for each download.
  • Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on the libtorrent engine.

Built with purpose. Every project here makes something a little more useful, one scratch at a time.
