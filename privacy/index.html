Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how I handle information in connection with my websites and mobile applications. I am committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a clear understanding of my data practices.

The core principle: I believe in privacy by design. My applications do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

1. Information collection

Mobile applications

My mobile applications are designed to be fully functional without requiring you to provide any personal information.

  • No PII: I do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No tracking: I do not track your usage, behavior, or metrics within the apps.
  • No account required: No registration or login is necessary to use the app features.

Website

My websites use Google Analytics to help me understand how visitors interact with the site. This helps me improve the user experience and monitor general traffic patterns.

  • Google Analytics uses “cookies” to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior information in an anonymous form.
  • The information generated by the cookie about your use of the website (including your IP address) is transmitted to Google.
  • I do not merge any personally identifiable information with the non-personally identifiable information collected through Google Analytics.
  • The games you can play in your browser on eightyfour.ca (Konbini Quest and Delve) are part of the website. Google Analytics counts visits to a game’s page and clicks on links to play a game or download a file. Konbini Quest sends nothing from inside the game (answers, moves, scores). What Delve reports is described next.

Delve in your browser

When you play Delve in your browser, the game reports what happens in it to Google Analytics, so I can see how people play and where the game is too hard or too easy. The Nintendo DS version sends nothing.

  • What is reported: milestones, such as starting a run, the depth you reach, a boss you meet or beat, a boon you take, an upgrade you buy, how a run ends and how long it took, and an error if the game stops. Every report also carries the game’s version.
  • The save ID: the first time you save, the game gives that save a random identifier (a UUID). It is stored inside your save, in your browser’s local storage on this site, and it is sent with every report, so that reports from the same save over time can be seen as coming from one player. It is a random number: it holds no name, email address or other detail about you. It does persist, though, and Google may treat it as personal data, so I am telling you.
  • It follows the save: if you export a save and import it again, in this or another browser, it is the same save and keeps its ID. Erasing a save slot ends that slot’s ID; the next save in it gets a new one. A Nintendo DS ignores the ID.
  • What is never sent: the name you type into the game, or anything else you type.
  • I do not sell or share it: although this data is anonymous, I do not sell it or share it with anyone else. It goes to Google Analytics, which collects it for me, and no one else receives it.
  • Your choices: erase a save slot, or clear this site’s data in your browser, and the ID is gone. Blocking Google Analytics in your browser stops the reports.

Contact form

The contact form on pandymic.com asks for your name, email address, a subject and a message. They are collected only to send me your message and to let me reply to you.

  • Not stored, shared or sold: the message is passed to Mailgun, the email service that delivers it to my inbox, and is not kept anywhere else.
  • Spam check and summary: before it reaches me, the subject and message are analysed by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, which acts as a processor: it flags likely spam and writes a short summary, shown only to me. The fields that exist to collect personal information, your name and email address, are never sent to Anthropic, and email and IP addresses written inside the message are masked first. Anything else you write in the message itself is part of what is analysed. Nothing is sent back to you, and every message is delivered whatever the result. A draft stays in your own browser (local storage, not a cookie) until you send or discard it.
  • Spam protection: before the form is shown, your browser solves a small puzzle and Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you’re human. Turnstile is run by Cloudflare under its own privacy policy.
  • Analytics: sending the form records an event in Google Analytics with a random reference number, the same one that appears in the email. Your name, address and message are never sent to Google.

2. Data usage

Since I do not collect personal data within the apps, there is no data to sell, trade, or rent to third parties. The anonymous traffic data collected via the website is used solely for internal optimization and performance monitoring.

3. Third-party services

While my apps do not track you, they may contain links to external websites or services. Once you leave the app or website, you are subject to the privacy policies of those external entities. I encourage you to review the privacy statements of any third-party service you visit.

4. Children’s privacy

My services are not directed to children under the age of 13. Because I do not collect any personal information from any of my users, I do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Changes to this policy

I may update my Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. I encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how I am protecting your information.
