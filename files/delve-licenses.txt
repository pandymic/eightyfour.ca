DELVE: LICENCES AND CREDITS
==========================
https://eightyfour.ca/delve/

Delve is free homebrew for the Nintendo DS by pandymic. It is an independent
fan-made game, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo. "Nintendo DS" is a
trademark of Nintendo. Its Android version is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Google; "Android" is a trademark of Google LLC.

This file lists every third-party piece in Delve with its author and licence,
and carries the notices that the libraries linked into the ROM, the libraries
bundled in the browser version (https://eightyfour.ca/delve/play/) and the
libraries in the Android version ask for. The art and music are the same in all
three versions.

The tracker modules' 16-bit samples were reduced to 8-bit to fit the DS's
memory, and some samples were resampled for cleaner playback; the notes and
arrangements are unchanged. "Public domain" is The Mod Archive's licence
attribution: the composer has released the module to the public domain
(https://modarchive.org/index.php?faq-licensing).
