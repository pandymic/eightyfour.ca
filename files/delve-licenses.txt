DELVE: LICENCES AND CREDITS
==========================
https://eightyfour.ca/delve/

Delve is free homebrew for the Nintendo DS by pandymic. It is an independent
fan-made game, not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo. "Nintendo DS" is a
trademark of Nintendo.

This file lists every third-party piece in Delve with its author and licence,
and carries the notices that the libraries linked into the ROM ask for.

  https://opengameart.org/content/calm-and-simple-title-music   (title, menus)

  https://opengameart.org/content/wander-in-a-dungeon           (The Dungeon)

  https://opengameart.org/content/evil-boss-music               (bosses)

  https://modarchive.org/index.php?request=view_by_moduleid&query=184094   (The Sunken Sewers)
Ambient Starfield, Drozerix, public domain
  https://modarchive.org/index.php?request=view_by_moduleid&query=174160   (The Crystal Caverns)
Crusaders, JAM, public domain
  https://modarchive.org/index.php?request=view_by_moduleid&query=177809   (The Molten Depths)

The tracker modules' 16-bit samples were reduced to 8-bit to fit the DS's
memory, and some samples were resampled for cleaner playback; the notes and
arrangements are unchanged. "Public domain" is The Mod Archive's licence
attribution: the composer has released the module to the public domain
(https://modarchive.org/index.php?faq-licensing).

the effects, the flames, the lava and murk textures and the placeholder icons
were drawn for Delve in the same palette. The sound effects are synthesized
for Delve.
