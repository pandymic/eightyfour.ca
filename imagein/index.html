Imagein

Shrink images to share.

21 MB → 1.9 MB

Big photos, light JPEGs

Phone cameras take photos several megabytes in size. Imagein scales them to 1920 pixels on the longest side and about 350 KB, which is plenty for any screen, and quick to send by email, chat or anywhere else.

  • Batch: pick up to 20 images, review the smaller copies and their sizes, then save.
  • Attach from other apps: choose Imagein in the system file picker (in Gmail, for example) and the smaller versions are attached instead.
  • Private by default: location, date and camera details are stripped unless you choose to keep them.
  • No permissions needed, light and dark themes, and built for TalkBack and large text.
