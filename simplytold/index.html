Simply Told

A focused audiobook player built for long-form audio.

Coming soon for Android

See it in actionJoin beta test

Press play and keep listening

Point Simply Told at a folder of audio and it becomes a book: the cover, the author, every track and every chapter, in order. Pick up where you left off, a week or a month later, down to the second.

The waveform over the cover isn’t a loop. It follows the narrator’s voice, worked out from the audio file itself, and it never asks for your microphone.

The books here are public-domain LibriVox recordings.

Made for long books

  • Chapters, wherever they live. One chapter per file, or one huge M4B with markers inside: previous and next skip by chapter either way, and the book reads as one continuous list.
  • Every chapter keeps its place. Finished, in progress or untouched, at a glance, and a tap jumps straight to one.
  • Rewind and skip ahead by your own interval, from 5 to 60 seconds.
  • Speed and volume, your way. Quick steps, or fine-tune the speed in steps of 0.05×.
  • Undo. Skipped too far by accident? One tap puts you back.
  • Bookmarks with your own labels, for the passage you want to hear again.
  • Ends at. The player tells you when the chapter and the book finish, at the speed you’re listening.

Your whole library

  • Choose one folder and every book in it shows up, however deep it’s filed: author, series, book.
  • Tidy by itself. A series of one is folded into its author, and CD 1, CD 2 and so on play as one book.
  • Search the whole library by book or folder name.
  • Progress on every cover. Books you’ve started show how far along you are, and the one that’s playing is marked.
  • History keeps your last ten books, each where you left it, at the speed you were listening.

Fall asleep to it

  • A sleep timer from 10 minutes to 4 hours, or any length you like.
  • Still awake? Give the phone a shake and the timer starts over.
  • It keeps counting. Lock the phone or switch to another app, and the book still stops on time.
  • Wake up where you drifted off. The activity log keeps a record of what played, per book, so you can find your place again.

Light or dark

Simply Told follows your phone’s theme, or picks one for good in Settings. Night listening gets navy and amber; daytime gets paper white.

Here it’s playing Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland, chapter by chapter.

Everywhere you listen

  • Android Auto. Your current book, your recent ones and your library are on the car’s screen, with covers. Pick one and it plays, without touching the phone.
  • Lock screen, headphones and Bluetooth controls all work, chapter-aware.
  • The formats you have: MP3, M4B, M4A, AAC, OGG, FLAC and WAV, plus M3U and PLS playlists.
  • Covers come from wherever you keep them: inside the audio file, or an image in the book’s folder.
  • In your language: English, French (Canada), Polish and Japanese.
  • Private by design. No account and no streaming: it plays the files already on your phone, and they stay there.

Help test Simply Told

Simply Told is in closed testing on Google Play. Testers get each new version before it’s out, and tell me what to fix before anyone else hears it. Long books, big libraries and Android Auto are all welcome.

  • What you need: an Android phone or tablet, and the Google account you use on Google Play.
  • Ask to join with the button below. I add your account to the test and email you the link to accept; it can take a few days.
  • Install Simply Told from Google Play as usual once you’ve accepted. New test versions arrive as ordinary updates.
  • Leave any time with “Leave the program” on the test’s Google Play page.
