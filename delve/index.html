Free for the Nintendo DS

An endless descent.

Download for DS

Fight, choose, fall, descend again

An action roguelike for the Nintendo DS in the spirit of Hades. Fight your way room to room down an endless isometric dungeon, choose your next door by its reward, die, grow stronger and go back down.

  • Four areas, four bosses, then the descent goes on, harder each time.
  • Sword, daggers, dash and a screen-clearing blast.
  • 13 boons per run, 15 permanent upgrades, and waypoints to skip ahead.
  • Three save slots, each with its own progress, run history and settings.
  • SNES-era pixel art in a 3D isometric world, across both DS screens, lit by torches, campfires and lava that throw sparks and smoke.
  • Free homebrew: one .nds file, for a flashcart, TWiLight Menu++ or an emulator.

In motion

The Dungeon, depth 6: orcs, mages and a warlock. The sword comes in combos, the dash gives you a moment of invulnerability, daggers fly at range, and every hit fills the blast meter; a full meter releases a ring that hits everything nearby.

The loop

Every run starts at a campsite in the woods. Visit the crypt to descend, the shrine to spend gold on permanent upgrades, or a training dummy to practise combos.

  • Clear the room, then pick one of three boons that last the run.
  • Choose a door. Each shows its reward, gold, healing or treasure, on the door and on the touch screen.
  • Every tenth depth is a boss. Beat one to open a waypoint to the next area.
  • Die, or abandon the run. The summary shows what ended it, and the gold you earned goes toward upgrades.

Four areas, four bosses

Rooms are generated from a seed, in squares, crosses and rings, rooms split by a chasm, ragged caves and lava rivers, with pits, spike and fire traps, saw blades, explosive barrels and floors that crack and give way. After the fourth area they repeat, harder.

  • The Dungeon

    Depths 1 to 10. Torch-lit halls full of orcs, mages, brutes, archers and stone idols. The boss is the Orc Warlord.

  • The Sunken Sewers

    Depths 11 to 20. Mossy stone and murky channels, with shamans who summon slimes. The boss is the Slime King.

  • The Crystal Caverns

    Depths 21 to 30. Glowing crystals and chasms, bats, burrowing crabs and crystals that fire shards. The boss is the Cavern Yeti.

  • The Molten Depths

    Depths 31 to 40. Basalt, lava rivers and cracking floor, with fire slimes, imps and magma brutes. The boss is the Magma Lord, a dragon.

Face the bosses

Every tenth depth is a boss, and each one fights differently. The boss’s health bar sits on the touch screen.

  • The Orc Warlord: charges, rings and fans of fireballs.
  • The Slime King: leaps, bursts and splits into slimes.
  • The Cavern Yeti: hops, slams and crystal rain.
  • The Magma Lord, a dragon: fire waves, and lava that floods the room’s edges.

Grow stronger

  • 13 boons per run, most of which stack: Keen Blade, Wide Arc, Twin Daggers, Piercing, Quick Draw, Flame Dash, Shock Dash, Fleet Foot, Overcharge, Big Bang, Vampiric, Thick Skin and Gold Rush.
  • 15 permanent upgrades at the shrine: more hearts and damage, a faster dash, an extra dagger, more gold, Second Wind, and two three-step paths, the Hunter’s and the Warden’s.
  • Waypoints. Beat an area’s boss and a run can start in the next area, with free boons to make up for the skipped rooms.
  • Three save slots. Choose a save after the title: each slot shows its best depth, runs, time played and upgrades, keeps its own run history and options, and the game remembers the one you played last.
  • Records. Best depth, runs, kills, bosses slain, time played and a history of your last runs.

Made for the DS

The 3D world fills the top screen; the touch screen shows the room, your stats and the door choices. Move with the D-pad, swing with A, dash with B, throw a dagger with Y and release the blast with X. Zoom in or out with the shoulder buttons.

There is a left-handed layout, an auto-attack option and full touch control of every menu. The soundtrack is tracker music, mixed to sound clean on the DS’s own speakers.

How to play

  • On a DSi or 3DS with TWiLight Menu++: copy the file to your SD card and launch it from the menu.
  • On a DS or DS Lite with a flashcart: copy the file to the cart’s SD card and start it from the cart’s menu.
  • On a computer or phone: open it in the melonDS emulator.
  • Your saves are written beside the ROM as delve-1.sav to delve-3.sav. Keep those files when you update; a new version picks up where the last left off.

Delve 2.5.0 has been played on a Nintendo 3DS running custom firmware and TWiLight Menu++, and runs in melonDS. Testing on a Nintendo DS Lite with an R4 flashcart is coming soon. Licences and credits, also as a text file.
