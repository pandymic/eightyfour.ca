eightyfour.ca is the home for my personal software projects: web and native apps purpose-built to serve specific needs in my personal and professional life. I build them to scratch my own itch, and occasionally share them with the world for the greater good.

More about me at my personal site, pandymic.com, or over on GitHub.

Philosophy

Everything here starts as a solution to a personal or professional friction. Whether it ships as a native Android or iOS app or a progressive web app, the goal is always utility over complexity.

Built with purpose. Every project here makes something a little more useful, one scratch at a time.
